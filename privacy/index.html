BoloCart Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

BoloCart is a WhatsApp order-confirmation assistant built and operated by Xenova Solutions, Block H2, 2 Abdul Haque Road, Phase 2, Johar Town, Lahore, Pakistan. It helps online stores confirm cash-on-delivery orders and answer customer questions on WhatsApp. This policy explains what BoloCart processes and why.

What we process

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the messages you send to a store that uses BoloCart.
  • Order details the store shares with BoloCart to confirm your order: order number, first name, total and delivery phone number.
  • Message delivery status and technical logs needed to run and secure the service.

How we use it

  • To send you an order confirmation, record your reply (confirm or cancel), and update the store's order.
  • To answer questions about products and delivery. Questions may be processed by an AI model to draft a reply.
  • To pass refunds, complaints and anything the assistant cannot answer to the store's team.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the store you ordered from and the service providers that run BoloCart: Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) and Cloudflare (hosting, database and AI processing).

Retention

Order and message records are kept only as long as needed to confirm orders and resolve questions, and no longer than 90 days, unless the store is legally required to keep them longer.

Your choices and data deletion

You can reply "stop" at any time and BoloCart will not message you again for that store. To request a copy or deletion of your data, email services@xenovasolutions.com with the phone number used on WhatsApp and, if you have it, the order number. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Security

Messages are received over HTTPS and every request from WhatsApp is checked against Meta's signature before it is processed. Access to stored data is limited to Xenova Solutions staff who operate the service.
