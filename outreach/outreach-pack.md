# BoloCart — outreach pack (Pakistani e-commerce brands)

## Offer
- **Problem:** COD parcels that are fake, impulse or unreachable come back at the brand's cost. DHL Pakistan puts the average
  return-to-origin rate at about 18–20% (roughly 1 in 5 COD parcels).
- **Product:** Urdu WhatsApp confirmation on every COD order before dispatch. Buttons, Roman Urdu, Urdu script and
  (next) voice notes. Answers product and "where is my order" questions; refunds/complaints go to the brand's team.
  Orders are tagged Confirmed / Cancel requested / No reply in the store.
- **Difference vs. existing Shopify COD apps (e.g. Confirmify, CODSaver):** conversational Urdu + voice notes,
  hosted in Pakistan, custom integration for non-Shopify stacks, and reporting on parcels held back.
- **Pilot:** free 30 days on one store or category. Brand uses its own WhatsApp Business account and pays Meta's
  per-message charges directly; Xenova charges nothing during the pilot.
- **After pilot (suggested):** PKR 25,000–75,000/month by order volume, or PKR 3–5 per confirmed order.
